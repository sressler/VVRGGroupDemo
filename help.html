NIST Group Demo — Help

This VRML demo was created to show off many projects in the VVRG group in 1998ish. It was never shown outside of NIST but was a substantial effort. Hope you enjoy!

A walk-through VRML world from NIST's Visualization & Virtual Reality Group, played here in the X_ITE browser.

Viewpoints

The world has many preset camera positions (viewpoints) that take you straight to each exhibit.

  • List of viewpoints: right-click in the 3D view and choose Viewpoints from the menu. Pick any one to fly there.
  • Step through them: the ◀ View and View ▶ buttons in the header go to the previous and next viewpoint, a quick guided tour. Page Up and Page Down do the same.
  • Reset view (or Home) returns to the first viewpoint. End jumps to the last one.
  • ⌂ Hall reloads the main hall (index.wrl) from the start, for example after you've followed a link into another world or dropped in a file.

The rooms

The building was adapted from the Virtual Music Museum and redecorated with the group's projects. Its rooms are spread over three floors joined by an elevator and stairs.

  • Anthropometry (AnthroKids) room: VRML models of children and a child in an air-bag car seat, from NIST's anthropometric data on children. Click the pictures on the wall to play their videos, including a baby movie and an air-bag test with sound. Click a picture again to replay it.
  • Anthropometric Landmarks of the Head: a scanned head in a glass case that slowly turns. The white dots mark standard measurement landmarks on the head and face.
  • The stairs and the falling man: click the human figure at the top of the steps and he's pushed down the stairs, tumbling under simulated physics, with a “D'oh!”.
  • WebMetrics room: displays for the WebVIP, WebCAT and WebSAT web-usability tools. Their signs link to the project pages.
  • NIRVE: a 3D visualization of search results (concept clusters on a sphere). The sign links to the publication.
  • Robot and miter saw: animated machinery models. Click parts of the robot to set them moving.
  • Credits room: photos of the group's members on the walls. Click a photo to open that person's page. The Credits plaque opens the full demo credits in this panel.

The elevator

The elevator works and takes you between all three floors.

  • Call it: click the call button beside the elevator door on your floor. The doors chime and open when it arrives.
  • Ride it: step inside. Music starts and the camera rides along with the car. Press a floor button to go up or down. Each button plays a short announcement for its floor.
  • The car moves, stops, and opens its doors at the floor you chose. If you press another button while it's moving or the doors are cycling, it goes there next.

Tips

  • Info panel: the Info panel button shows or hides this side panel, where links from the world open.
  • View other files: drag a .wrl, .x3dv, .x3d, .gltf or .glb file onto the page to view it. Files it refers to are found in this folder, or you can drop them along with it. ⌂ Hall brings the demo back.
  • More options: the right-click menu also has navigation modes, rendering options and full-screen.
  • Dead links: some links point to old NIST pages that no longer exist, and they'll open an error page.
